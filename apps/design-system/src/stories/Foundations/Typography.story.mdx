    ### Overview

    Our typography system is built on Montserrat, Barlow Condensed, and Inter, chosen for their modern, approachable, and highly readable qualities that reflect The Groom Squad’s professional yet welcoming tone.

    **Key characteristics:**
    - **Montserrat** – Brand, navigation, headings (modern, clean, professional)
    - **Barlow Condensed** – Tagline, eyebrow (contemporary, distinctive, approachable)
    - **Inter** – Body text (highly readable, clear, digital-optimized)
